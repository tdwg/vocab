# Vocabulary Maintenance Standard

<p class="lead">The TDWG Vocabulary Maintenance Standard consists of a single document: the <a href="/specification">Vocabulary Maintenance Specification</a>. That document describes the processes used to modify TDWG vocabularies and their associated documents.</p>

## Getting started

* [Vocabulary Maintenance Specification](/specification)
* [GitHub repository](https://github.com/tdwg/vocab): where the Vocabulary Maintenance Standard is maintained. This is the place to go if you want to become involved in improving the standard.
